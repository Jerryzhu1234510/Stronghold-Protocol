## 35. Intentional local golden changes

Compared against upstream commit `c24e17eec3af1e6dedfa9935a6d15245cf2f4586`; the original 288-scenario corpus is saved outside the release at `../official-golden-before-compat-20261016/golden/`. The local corpus has 291 scenarios. This is an intentional custom baseline, not a claim that upstream results stayed identical.

## Why results move

- The maintainer retained the old shop-form Clementia, Sakiko and Makoto. The extra shop entry changes sorted roster groupings, bond representatives, common Boss/helper lineups, random shop draws and match lineups; the two DIY operators add three corpus groups. Existing official operator records and official Clementia skills win when overlapping.
- The maintainer explicitly retained the upper water-map Boss passage. The adapter preserves both patrol directions, waits and teleports, closes the patrol at its first movement checkpoint, and avoids leaking through the goal sentinel. This affects water Boss fields and match outcomes.
- Official 0.3.0 shared-stock and skill rules remain the default. PvP-specific economy, cap and projection fixes stay scoped to that retained optional mode. The whole local baseline was regenerated after these intentional gameplay differences; no golden was relaxed to hide a nondeterministic result.
